# Visual style: haunted answering machine

- Palette: near-black plum background, warm candle-gold accent, bone-white text. Emotion colors come only from `src/data/emotionStyle.ts`.
- Color is never the only cue: each emotion also has a glyph.
- Motifs: film grain and vignette, flickering title, tape reels, red REC light, messages that "bleed" in.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; text contrast must stay readable on the dark background.
- Touch targets at least 44px. Phones use a bottom sheet for the room panel.
- Tone of copy: short, quiet, a little eerie; Chinese and English should read equally natural.
